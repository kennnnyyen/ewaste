# Material card graphics

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Active assets are modern geometric illustrations with matte clay surfaces and emerald, ivory, charcoal, and copper colours.

Files in `public/materials/` (`pcbs-art.webp`, `cpus-art.webp`, `ram-art.webp`, and `gpus-art.webp`) are 640 px wide, preserve transparency, and are served with responsive Next.js Image sizing. Original PNGs remain in the image-generation output directory. Earlier realistic assets remain as unused alternatives.
